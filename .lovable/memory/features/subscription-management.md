---
name: Subscription fees
description: Kiduka plan prices — free tier, base, branch, per-item overage (products/customers TSh 1,000 each)
type: feature
---
- Free: 2 staff (owner counts), 20 products, 10 customers.
- Base (HQ): TSh 30,000/month — 5 staff, 100 products, 200 customers.
- Branch: TSh 20,000/month each, max 3 staff per branch.
- Overage charged automatically on the next bill: each extra product TSh 1,000, each extra customer TSh 1,000, extra staff TSh 5,000 (staff amount not yet confirmed by user).
- Super admin edits prices on /billing-plans; values live in billing_settings.
