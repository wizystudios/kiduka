---
name: Subscription Fees
description: Kiduka monthly pricing rules
type: feature
---
- Base TSh 30,000/month includes 3 employees free.
- Business size: large = 5,000+ active products, otherwise small.
- Extra employee: TSh 2,000 (small) / TSh 500 (large).
- Branch: TSh 10,000 (small) / TSh 15,000 (large); each branch fee covers 3 branch employees, extras pay the extra-employee fee.
- Sokoni: TSh 50,000/month. Extra product/customer beyond limits: TSh 1,000 each.
